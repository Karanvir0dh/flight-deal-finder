# Deal Scoring

Scores are 0 to 100:

- Absolute affordability: 25
- Discount versus median: 25
- Historical rarity: 15
- Itinerary quality: 15
- Booking confidence: 10
- Price momentum: 10

Penalties cover self-transfer, separate tickets, long duration, basic economy, missing baggage, stale or unverified fares, and provider disagreement. Every alert includes a human-readable explanation.
